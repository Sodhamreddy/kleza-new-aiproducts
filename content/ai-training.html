Challenge

New caregiver onboarding took 2 weeks because training materials were scattered across documents and institutional knowledge held by senior staff.

Approach

FAQ

Questions about AI training.

LLM fine-tuning is the process of training a large language model on your specific data — so it learns your terminology, workflows, and context rather than relying on generic public training data. The result is AI that answers domain-specific questions accurately instead of hallucinating plausible-sounding but incorrect responses.

The ideal starting dataset includes operational documentation (SOPs, training materials), communication records, and any structured data your team produces regularly. Kleza conducts a data discovery audit first — we will tell you whether your existing data is sufficient and what gaps exist before any commitment.

All healthcare data is processed under signed Data Processing Agreements and HIPAA-compliant protocols. We de-identify patient data before any training use, use secure isolated environments for training runs, and provide full data handling documentation on request.

A focused AI assistant typically deploys in 6-10 weeks from data discovery to go-live. Full LLM fine-tuning projects range from 8-16 weeks depending on data complexity and evaluation requirements.
