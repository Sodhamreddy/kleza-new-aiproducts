WHY KLEZA

What it’s like to work here.

01

Meaningful work, not busy work

Every project at Kleza connects to a real outcome — a caregiver who has better tools, a healthcare organisation that spends less time on paperwork and more on patients. We are deliberate about the work we take on, and that makes the work worth taking on. You will know why your work matters here.

02

Craft taken seriously

Whether you are a developer, a designer, a content strategist, or an AI engineer, Kleza invests in doing things properly. We don’t ship things we’re not proud of. We encourage deep expertise, build time for learning into our culture, and hold a high bar for quality — because our clients depend on it.

03

People across two continents, one culture

Kleza operates across Hyderabad and Kansas, and our culture doesn’t split at the timezone line. The Hyderabad team is where most of our engineering, design, marketing, and operational delivery happens. The USA team handles client strategy and healthcare operations. Both teams have full visibility, equal voice, and shared ownership of the work we produce.

LIFE AT KLEZA

Two offices. One team.

Hyderabad, India

Hyderabad — where most of the work gets built.

Our Madhapur, Hyderabad office is where Kleza’s engineering, design, digital marketing, and AI development teams are based. It’s a fast-moving, technically ambitious environment — a place where people grow quickly because the work demands it.

Overland Park, Kansas

Kansas, USA — where client strategy lives.

Our Overland Park, Kansas office manages client relationships, healthcare operations delivery, and USA-based strategy. If you’re based in the USA, you’ll work closely with healthcare organisations and enterprise clients — under HIPAA-compliant processes — who depend on Kleza’s services every day.
