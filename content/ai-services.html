Operating Model

How Kleza deploys AI — responsibly and precisely.

01

Data Integrity

Every model we build starts with a thorough data audit. We identify gaps, inconsistencies, and compliance risks before a single training run begins. Your model is only as good as the data behind it — we make sure both are excellent.

02

Human Oversight

No Kleza AI system operates without a human review layer. Model outputs are evaluated by domain experts before deployment, and escalation paths to human agents are built into every assistant we deploy.

03

Continuous Evaluation

Post-deployment, Kleza monitors model performance against accuracy and relevance benchmarks. Models are updated as your data evolves. AI that doesn’t improve over time is a liability — ours improves by design.

Kleza offers two core AI services: Business-Specific AI Training — where we fine-tune large language models on your operational data — and AI Support Assistants, which are 24/7 conversational AI systems trained on your knowledge base. Both are available for healthcare and enterprise organisations.
