Safety Architecture

The AI handles what it knows. Humans handle what it doesn’t.

Every Kleza AI assistant operates with a built-in confidence threshold. When a query falls below that threshold — because it is ambiguous, sensitive, or outside the assistant’s training scope — it escalates immediately to a human agent with full conversation context. The AI never guesses on high-stakes queries.

Step 01

Query received

A staff member, client, or family member sends a question through any connected channel.

Step 02

AI assesses confidence

The assistant scores its certainty and screens for patient-safety and sensitivity flags.

High confidence

AI responds instantly with a source reference.

Low or safety-flagged

Instant escalation to a human agent with full context.

For healthcare clients, clinical questions involving patient safety always escalate regardless of confidence score.
