Every hour your systems are down, your team is paying for it — even if you never see the invoice.

Inaccessible scheduling systems mean missed caregiver assignments. Network failures mean staff can’t access patient records or care plans. Software issues mean your team improvises instead of working — and improvising in healthcare operations creates real risk.

The average IT incident costs a small business $10,000 in staff downtime — before any hardware or recovery costs. Kleza’s remote IT team means issues are resolved before they become incidents, and incidents are resolved before they become crises.
