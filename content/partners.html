THE PROGRAMME

Find the right kind of partnership for your organisation

Tier 01 · Technology Partner

Technology Partner

For software vendors, SaaS platforms, and technology providers whose products complement or integrate with Kleza’s AI and digital services.

  • Joint integration development and co-marketing opportunities
  • Listed in the Kleza IVNA Marketplace as a verified technology partner
  • Access to Kleza’s technical team for integration support
Apply as a Technology Partner
Tier 02 · Referral Partner

Referral Partner

For consultants, agencies, brokers, and professional service providers who regularly interact with healthcare or enterprise organisations that could benefit from Kleza’s services.

  • Referral fee structure for qualified introductions that convert
  • Co-branded proposal materials and case studies
  • Dedicated Kleza partner contact for every referred opportunity
Apply as a Referral Partner
Tier 03 · Reseller Partner

Reseller Partner

For managed service providers, staffing firms, and digital agencies that want to offer Kleza’s services under their own brand or as part of their existing client packages.

  • White-label service delivery options available
  • Volume-based pricing for resold service packages
  • Priority support and a dedicated account manager
