The Model

How our outsourcing model works.

Not a shared resource pool. Dedicated people, trained for your operational reality, working under signed compliance agreements.

01

Dedicated Teams, Not Shared Resources

Your outsourced team members work exclusively for your organisation during agreed hours. They learn your systems, your culture, and your way of working. You are not buying slots in a shared pool — you are gaining dedicated people.

02

Domain Training Before Day One

Every team member Kleza places is trained on your industry’s operational context before they start. For healthcare clients, that means US home care scheduling norms, HIPAA awareness, caregiver communication standards, and your specific software systems.

03

Compliance Assurance Built In

Kleza maintains signed confidentiality and data handling agreements for all outsourcing engagements. For healthcare clients, all team members handling client or patient-adjacent data operate under HIPAA-compliant protocols.
