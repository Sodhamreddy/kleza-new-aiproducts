A trigger is what starts a run. The flat-left shape means “nothing happens until something calls this”.
A node is one step. It has settings, and — if it talks to another company — a stored credential. Our Anthropic key lives there, and nowhere else.
A run (n8n calls it an execution) is one journey through the workflow, kept with everything that went in and came out. That is how you debug a bad answer.
The dashed line means “this is a part the step uses” — the model, its memory, its tools — not the next step.
